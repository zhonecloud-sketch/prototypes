## Original production prompt

```text
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: raster portrait atlas for a late Han /
Three Kingdoms mobile strategy game. Create one single 2048x2048 sheet divided
into exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of equal square portrait cells, touching edge
to edge, no borders, labels, writing, logos or watermark. Each cell is a
separate realistic, naturalistic head-and-shoulders oil-painted character
portrait, finely detailed face, silk robes or period lamellar armor, cinematic
soft daylight, restrained colors, dark neutral background, historically
plausible Chinese people circa AD 190-220, never modern celebrity likenesses.
The 16 cells in row-major order: Cao Cao (wily middle-aged narrow eyes black
beard dark robe); Liu Bei (gentle middle-aged long ears thin moustache green
robe); Sun Quan (young resolute ruler auburn-black short beard dark crimson
robe); Guan Yu (powerful mature general long black beard green robe); Zhang Fei
(rugged broad-faced black-bearded warrior); Zhao Yun (handsome young
clean-shaven silver-armored general); Zhuge Liang (calm scholarly man small
beard cloth scholar cap); Zhou Yu (elegant young clean-shaven red-armored
commander); Sima Yi (older calculating narrow face gray-black beard black
scholar hat); Lu Bu (tall fierce young warrior elaborate Han headdress armor);
Dong Zhuo (heavy older sinister bearded lord); Yuan Shao (dignified wealthy
middle-aged leader fine purple-brown robe); Sun Jian (forty-year-old determined
warrior red scarf and Han armor); Ma Chao (young clean-shaven martial man pale
armor); Huang Zhong (elderly strong gray-bearded veteran in bronze armor);
anonymous officer placeholder (face in deep shadow, plain Han helmet, neutral
dark background). Portraits are artistic interpretations, not purported
historical likenesses. Every head fully contained within its cell, consistent
head size and shoulder crop. Photorealistic proportions and materials, not
anime, not cel shading, not 3D render.
```
