```text
Use case: historical-scene.
Asset type: final animated army sprite atlas for a premium late Han tactical
strategy game, PNG with genuine transparent alpha background, exactly 2048 x
1024 pixels, exactly four equal columns and two equal rows, eight 512 x 512
square cells.
Primary request: every cell depicts THE SAME COMPLETE ARMY FORMATION OF
TWENTY-FOUR LATE HAN INFANTRY, arranged as exactly SIX small helmeted soldiers
ACROSS by FOUR orderly RANKS deep. Each formation includes the same twenty-four
infantry with spears and shields, and two modest crimson silk standards carried
within the ranks. Make a readable mass of small soldiers; never a lone enlarged
soldier. The count, scale, formation footprint, camera angle, center position,
costume and composition are consistent across all eight cells.
Animation order: top row left to right shows four consecutive group marching
poses, all twenty-four soldiers moving together in a coherent march cycle.
Bottom row left to right shows four consecutive coordinated group attack poses:
ready with spears held, advance as a formation, synchronized spear thrust,
recover into formation. Only limb and spear poses change between frames. Keep
the same army and two standards in every frame.
Grid layout: exactly four equal columns by two equal rows. Each complete
formation is centered inside its own equal square cell with generous transparent
margins on all sides; no soldier, spear, banner or shadow touches or crosses a
cell boundary. The group should occupy roughly the central two-thirds of each
cell, enabling reliable sprite cropping.
Style/medium: naturalistic finely painted 2D historical game sprites, matching
detailed late Han portraits, restrained worn iron armor, muted cloth, crimson
accents, authentic small shields and spears, no fantasy gear. Three-quarter
overhead tactical view from above, a consistent direction in all cells.
Lighting/mood: consistent soft sunlight from upper left, crisp readable soldier
silhouettes and ranks, polished premium art.
Transparency: truly transparent empty canvas and gaps around every formation,
preserve alpha; no colored backdrop, no ground, no base plate, no checkerboard
painted into the picture.
Constraints: twenty-four soldiers, six across and four ranks in EVERY cell;
entire army formation in EVERY cell; same scale; all eight formations separate
and completely contained.
Avoid: lone oversized soldier, hero portrait, merged overlapping frames, missing
ranks, varying soldier count, background scenery, UI, text, letters, labels,
numbers, border, grid lines, frame, watermark, modern objects, cartoon styling,
plastic 3D render.
```
