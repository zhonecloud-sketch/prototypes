## Original production prompt

```text
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: narrative event atlas for a historical
strategy game. Create ONE WIDE LANDSCAPE image with EXACTLY 2 COLUMNS and 2 ROWS
of four equally sized LANDSCAPE cells. Each cell's width is greater than its
height, roughly 3:2; overall canvas roughly 3:2. No borders, no gutters, no
frame, no margin, no text, labels, logos, watermarks or UI. Each scene is a
complete independent late Han Chinese narrative painting, fits fully within its
quadrant, and does not blend into the next cell. Naturalistic restrained painted
realism, detailed period architecture and robes, dark jade, warm brown,
charcoal, dusty ochre and muted gold palette matching existing RTK2 realistic
event art; subdued atmospheric light and soft brush texture. No cartoon, 3D,
fantasy, modern clothing or objects. Strict row-major assignments:
Top left: plague; a compassionate Han physician attends sick villagers under a
rural wooden shelter; several people cover their faces with plain cloth, baskets
of herbs and bamboo bowls; no modern medical equipment, no gore.
Top right: luminous comet or meteor with long pale gold streak over an ancient
Han town at dusk; astonished townspeople in robes gaze upward from a dirt street
between tiled-roof timber houses.
Bottom left: war; a late Han army deploys through a massive timber and earthen
city gate, mounted officer, disciplined infantry with spears, dark jade and
muted ochre banners with no writing, dust and muted dawn light.
Bottom right: occupation; new plain dark jade military banner being raised over
a captured city gate, weary armored soldiers and cautiously watching townspeople
below, smoky distant roofs, restrained victorious scene.
Preserve exactly four separate landscape scenes with equal cell sizes; clean
straight invisible seams, no decorative dividing lines.
```
