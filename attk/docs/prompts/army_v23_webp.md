## Original production prompt

```text
Create a transparent 4×4 sprite sheet of compact Han dynasty army formations,
each containing 10–14 soldiers, viewed from an elevated isometric angle. Red
clothes in the first two rows, blue clothes in the last two. Row 1 and 3 are
marching frames; row 2 and 4 are attacking frames. Match the existing painted
strategy game, use crisp readable silhouettes and clean separated cells, with
blank banners and no letters or numbers.
```
