```text
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: animated 2D raster sprite atlas for a
Han China strategy game. Transparent background. One single 1536x1024 image
divided into exactly 4 columns by 2 rows of equal 384x512 cells. In each cell
the same realistic late Han Chinese infantryman in dark iron lamellar armor, red
waist sash, tied hair under simple dark Han helmet, holding a spear, full body
from head to feet, 3/4 facing right. Consistent figure size, feet aligned at the
same y coordinate, each character fits fully inside his cell with generous
transparent margin, no cropped weapons crossing cell edges. Naturalistic
detailed painted realism, realistic anatomy, cloth and metal; NOT cartoon,
anime, plastic 3D render or pixel art. Row one: four consecutive phases of a
slow marching gait, spear upright, moving legs and slight arm motion, intended
as a repeating walk animation. Row two: four consecutive phases of a spear
thrust attack, ready, beginning thrust, extended thrust, recovering, intended as
an attack animation. Exactly 8 characters, no other objects, no text, numbers,
grid, borders or watermark. Genuine clear alpha transparency around all sprites.
```
