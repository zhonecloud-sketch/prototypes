## Original production prompt

```text
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: raster 2D terrain texture atlas used
inside an accurately drawn geographic map and a hex strategy map, not itself a
map. Create one 2048x1024 sheet of 8 square cells, exactly 4 columns by 2 rows,
touching edge to edge, no text, borders, labels, UI or watermark. Each square is
an overhead aerial orthographic photograph-like naturalistic painting,
consistent late Han China midday daylight, no isometric perspective, no 3D
render look. Row-major cells: 1 grassy dry fertile plains with subtle footpaths;
2 dense green broadleaf forest; 3 rolling foothills with grass and earth; 4 gray
rugged exposed rocky mountains; 5 blue-green river water with natural sandy
banks along opposing edges; 6 small earthen and timber military fort with walls
and gates seen directly from above; 7 small realistic late Han walled town with
courtyard roofs and gray tiled halls seen directly from above; 8 irrigated rice
paddies divided by narrow dikes. Ground fills every cell. Real fine natural
textures and historically plausible buildings, no modern infrastructure. Each
cell must work when cropped independently as a tiled raster game asset.
```
