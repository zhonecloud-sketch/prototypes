Multiplayer service

Start stays enabled until you issue Stop. Players can connect without this page or your computer staying open. Stop blocks new joins and wagers; accepted wagers still settle.

Sites keeps the API available on demand. It does not run a permanent background process. Players refresh the shared room automatically; an interrupted wager is settled on the next account or room request. These controls pause the game, while keeping account and operator services available.

Real-life RNG databases

Multiplayer always follows the weekly calendar of 2m-rng.txt, shared with real.html. It continues across weeks and loops at the end, labelled as repeated data. Imported databases below are for inspection and editing; they cannot replace or rewind the multiplayer stream.
