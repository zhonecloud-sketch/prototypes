Sign in with Google

The app identifies your account and saves your play-credit balance, pending wagers, and recent results in its private Google Drive app-data folder. This lets you restore your saved progress when you sign in again.

Google sign-in is optional. Google handles its own account selection and sign-in screens. Atelier Roulette does not receive your Google password. Its Drive permission covers this app’s private save data; it does not grant access to your ordinary Drive documents.

Drive saving requires your permission and an active connection. If access expires, the app asks you to reconnect. Leave one active Google bankroll session open at a time, and check the saved status before closing.
