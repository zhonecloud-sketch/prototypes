# Croupier audio

The game announcements were synthesized with [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl)
using the [Cori medium voice model](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/cf910fb/en/en_GB/cori/medium/MODEL_CARD).

The model uses recordings by Cori Samuel for LibriVox from the
[Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English TTS Dataset](https://www.openslr.org/109/)
by NVIDIA Corporation, licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The roulette dialogue was newly synthesized, assembled, and encoded for
Atelier Roulette. Creator credit for the resulting audio is embedded as zhone.
