Use case: historical-scene.

Lighting/mood: soft consistent relief illumination, restrained and legible
beneath map overlays.
Constraints: opaque continuous terrain canvas with no actual coastline, no exact

boundaries, no region divisions, no city markers, no roads or settlement
symbols. Keep the entire canvas inland terrain.
Avoid: labels, Chinese characters, words, numbers, UI, legend, compass rose,
grid, ornamental border, frame, watermark, modern objects, satellite photograph,
exaggerated fantasy mountains, isometric view.
