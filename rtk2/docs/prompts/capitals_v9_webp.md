Use case: historical-scene.
Asset type: final miniature provincial capital sprite atlas for a premium late

neighborhoods; distinguish the cities by regional architecture, layout and
silhouette. Imperial capital has formal palace courtyards; northern town has
rugged earth fortifications; central market has dense tiled roofs; river port
has docks and river-facing gates; southeastern city has canal bridges and paddy
details; western city fits a mountain valley; southern port has timber wharves;

Style/medium: naturalistic finely painted 2D historical miniatures, coherent
with detailed dark jade and gold late Han portraits, premium finished game
artwork with clear readable forms. Same sunlight direction and aerial camera
angle across all eight stamps.
Transparency: real transparent background around every stamp; no ground-colored
canvas, no painted checkerboard, no rectangular or circular base plate, no
pedestal. Only the compact city and integrated small regional terrain features
are opaque.
Constraints: all eight complete cities inside their equal cells; consistent
miniature scale and lighting; no modern buildings.
Avoid: UI, letters, labels, captions, Chinese writing, numbers, decorative
frame, borders, grid lines, watermark, tall modern towers, fantasy castles,
cartoon icons, plastic 3D render, oversized scene spilling into adjacent cells.
