## Original production prompt

```text
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: narrative event atlas for a historical
strategy game. Create ONE WIDE LANDSCAPE image with EXACTLY 2 COLUMNS and 2 ROWS
of four equally sized LANDSCAPE cells. Each cell's width is greater than its
height, roughly 3:2; overall canvas roughly 3:2. No borders, no gutters, no
frame, no margin, no text, labels, logos, watermarks or UI. Each scene is a
complete independent late Han Chinese narrative painting, fits fully within its
quadrant, and does not blend into the next cell. Naturalistic restrained painted
realism, detailed period architecture and robes, dark jade, warm brown,
charcoal, dusty ochre and muted gold palette matching existing RTK2 realistic
event art; subdued atmospheric light and soft brush texture. No cartoon, 3D,
fantasy, modern clothing or objects. Strict row-major assignments:
Top left: Xun Yu as a refined Han strategist, manipulating open scrolls on a low
wooden table; a symbolic period map without writing carries two small rival army
formations and plain jade and ochre standards; warm lamp-lit strategy chamber.
Top right: famous officer's death; sorrowful mourners in plain pale hemp Han
robes kneel around an empty dark helmet on a wooden altar, incense and subdued
flowers, soft dim light, solemn dignity and absolutely no body or gore.
Bottom left: Diaochan chain stratagem; an elegant young Chinese lady in
restrained rose-brown crossed Han robes stands between two rival Han lords, one
older broad lord in court robes and one handsome younger armored officer,
conflicting gazes in a richly shadowed period hall.
Bottom right: Hua Tuo medical book; elderly Han physician gently tending a
seated armored officer's arm while a bamboo-slip and stitched medical
manuscript, medicinal herbs, small wooden boxes and earthen bowls lie on a low
table; no readable text or modern objects.
Preserve exactly four separate landscape scenes with equal cell sizes; clean
straight invisible seams, no decorative dividing lines.
```
